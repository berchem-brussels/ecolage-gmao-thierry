# Timed usability test

Version 0.18.0 · The French text prevails (`docs/fr/RECETTE-USAGE.md`); this file summarises it.

Test with five staff members per entity (municipal administration and CPAS) who do not know the application (REC-03), timed against the ERG-02 objectives. It closes lot L1.

- Measured: O1, submitting a work request in under 60 seconds without training (from the home screen to the request number on screen); O2, following a request in two clicks from the home screen; O3 (observation, no threshold), submitting a request with a photo on an iPhone or Android phone.
- Participants anonymous (AC-1 to AC-5, CPAS-1 to CPAS-5), own account with the requester role, oral consent, no recording. No usual workplace set beforehand; the guided tour may open (noted, not timed).
- The facilitator reads the instructions (French and Dutch text in the French version, section 6) and does not help; after two minutes of blockage, they give the shortest possible hint and note « help ».
- Proposed thresholds per entity (to be confirmed in a workshop): O1, at least 4 out of 5 without help and a median time under 60 s; O2, at least 4 out of 5 in 2 clicks at most without help; O3, no blockage caused by the phone. A blockage is a defect (version 0.18.Z), a hesitation a remark for the next version.
- Measurement grid in section 7 of the French text. The test requests are cancelled afterwards with the reason « usability test ». The application does not time staff or count their clicks: measurement is done by hand.
